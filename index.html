I'm a Research Fellow in the Department of Physics at King's College London, working in the Attosecond Quantum Physics Laboratory group.

Currently

I'm currently working on the development of new methods to detect and control molecular chirality with ultrafast temporal resolution and enhanced efficiency. My work spans attosecond and ultrafast spectroscopy, structured light, non-Hermitian physics, analytical and numerical methods to characterize chiral objects.
