About me

I'm

I'm currently a UKRI HE Guarantee Marie-Curie Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Attosecond Quantum Physics Laboratory group at King's College London, working with Dr. Emilio Pisanty, on the project TopROCS, Topologically Protected Chiral Sensing. Before that, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Theory Department of MBI Berlin with Prof. Olga Smirnova and a graduate researcher with Prof. Misha Ivanov at HU Berlin and MBI, in Germany. I completed my MSc studies in HU, and my BSc in the University of Trieste. My research interests are time-resolved spectroscopy, chirality, structured light, non-Hermitian physics, plasmonics and analytical and numerical methods to describe quantum phenomena on the ultrafast timescale.
